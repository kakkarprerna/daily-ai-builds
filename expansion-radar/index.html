    How it works

    The formula behind the score

    Fixed weights, no model call, nothing sent anywhere. Every number below is printed so you can argue with it.

    Five weighted categories

    Usage depth & growth25%

    Seats used, feature breadth, and the growth trend, averaged.

    Organisational spread20%

    Teams beyond the original buyer, plus how engaged the exec sponsor is.

    Champion advocacy20%

    Number of advocacy signals seen, capped at three, plus NPS or CSAT.

    Buying signals20%

    What kind of feature requests are coming in, and whether they've expanded with you before.

    Timing fit15%

    How close the renewal or budget cycle sits. Closer generally means a cleaner window to ask.

    Which play gets picked

    Checked in this order, first match wins:

    1. Seat Expansion — idle seats sit at 15% or more, usage is growing, and the overall score clears 50.
    2. Cross-sell Adjacent Module — multiple teams are already in it, and the feature requests point outside the current plan.
    3. Tier Upgrade Conversation — the score is 80 or above and the renewal sits within six months.
    4. Reference-to-Expansion Bridge — they've agreed to a reference or case study, and the score clears 60.
    5. Nurture Play — none of the above. Keep the relationship warm, no ask yet.
    This is a decision-support heuristic built from CSM experience, not a prediction model. It doesn't read any live usage data, connect to any account, or store anything you enter. Every chip field lets you add your own wording if none of the presets fit, and a custom entry scores as neutral (0.5) for its category, since there's no built-in weight yet for something that didn't exist when the formula was written. Treat the score as a prompt to go check, not a verdict.
